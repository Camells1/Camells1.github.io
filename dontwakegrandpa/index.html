Features

What's in the house

🎙

He hears your real voice

Proximity voice chat for the crew, and Grandpa's noise meter listens to your actual microphone. Whisper near the armchair.

🥿

A proper Grandpa

He talks in his sleep, stomps round the furniture, squats to swat, throws a tantrum when he misses and laughs when he doesn't.

🎩

Dress the whole bean

Body, eyes, mouth, real 3D clothes, hats, capes, jetpacks. Then eight dances and eight emotes to show it off.

😈

Troll your friends

Shrink rays, helium balloons, pots of glue, alarm clocks, cream pies, and a frying pan for when words fail you.

🍌

Ragdolls and bananas

Whacked beans go flying. Flat beans get pumped back up. Banana peels work on absolutely everyone.

4

Beans, one room code

Play with up to 3 friends using a room code. No server to run. You can also practise on your own.
