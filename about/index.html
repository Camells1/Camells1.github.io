How we got here

From one shooter to five games

First Riftline

A tactical shooter for 1v1 and 2v2, because we wanted something we could play in a lunch break. Agents, abilities, a spike to plant. It set the tone for everything after it.

Then Shattercrown and Hollowtide

We wanted to see how far we could push co-op. An open-world RPG with eleven bosses, and an adventure game where the sea drains for a few minutes and you loot whatever is down there before it comes back.

Now The bean games

Don't Wake Grandpa and Night Shift Zoo. Smaller, funnier, built around voice chat and the noises people make when something goes wrong. Everyone is a bean.

Next Number six

In the oven. It has beans in it.

Questions people ask

The honest answers

Why is everything free?

Because we make these for our friends and charging them would be strange. There is no store, no battle pass and no ads. If that ever changes, it will be said out loud on this page first.

Is this a real company?

No. It is a small crew that ships games. The name is a joke that stuck.

Can I play on a Mac or a phone?

Not properly yet. The client is Windows 10 and 11 only. Four of the games have browser builds that work on ChromeOS and in Chrome or Edge on any machine, which covers most school laptops.

Something is broken. Where do I say so?

Open an issue on the game's GitHub repository. Include what you were doing and what happened. We read all of them and most fixes go out within a few days.

Can I help make them?

The repositories are public. Pull requests are welcome, and so is anyone who wants to test builds before they go out and tell us what feels wrong.
