Do I need an account to download?

No. You need one to play anything with other people, and making it takes about ten seconds on the account page.

Where does it install?

Into your user folder, not Program Files, so it does not ask for administrator rights. Games go in a subfolder next to the client.

How do I uninstall it?

Through Windows Settings, under Installed apps, same as anything else. Removing the client removes the games it installed.

My antivirus flagged it.

That happens with unsigned installers from small projects. The source is public on GitHub if you or someone you trust wants to look through it first.
