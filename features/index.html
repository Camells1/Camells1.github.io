The Camel Client

One app, every game

You install one thing. It keeps all five games up to date, signs you in once, and shows you which friends are around. Free, no ads, nothing to buy.

Get the client See the games
📦

Install once

Every game lives in the client. Pick one from the library, hit install, and it is on your machine a minute later. No separate installers, no hunting for the right download.

⚡

Always the newest build

Updates land in the background. You never join a match on an old version and you never have to think about patch notes before you play.

👤

One account everywhere

One Camel Studios login with a username#tag, same as Discord. Your name, your friends and your progress follow you into every game we make.

💬

Friends and presence

See who is online and what they are playing. Click a friend to drop into their lobby without copying codes around.

🎤

Voice chat built in

Proximity voice in the games that use it. In Don't Wake Grandpa the sleeping man actually hears your microphone, which is the whole joke.

🔑

Rooms with codes

Every co-op game makes a six-character room code. Send it to a friend, they paste it, you are in the same session. It works across the client and the browser builds.

🌎

On a Chromebook instead?

Four of the five games have a cut-down build that runs in a browser tab, with no install and no client. Smaller maps, fewer effects, no voice chat, but the same game and the same account. Good enough for a free period at school.

Getting started

Three steps and you are in

Download

Grab the client for Windows. It is one file and it installs in under a minute.

Make an account

Pick a username and a four-character tag. That is your name across every game.

Install a game

Open the library, pick something, press play. Send the room code to a friend.
